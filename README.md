# My Favorite Series: Avatar: The Last Airbender

## About the Series

**Avatar: The Last Airbender** is an animated series that aired on *Nickelodeon* from **2005 to 2008**. It follows Aang, the last surviving Airbender, on his journey to master all four elements and bring peace to a world at war.

> "Water. Earth. Fire. Air. Long ago, the four nations lived together in harmony."

## The Four Nations

1. Water Tribe
2. Earth Kingdom
3. Fire Nation
4. Air Nomads

## Main Characters

- **Aang** – the Avatar and last Airbender
- **Katara** – a Waterbender from the Southern Water Tribe
- **Sokka** – Katara's brother, a skilled warrior
- **Toph** – a blind but powerful Earthbender
- **Zuko** – the exiled prince of the Fire Nation
